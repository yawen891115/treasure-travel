      How the agent built this plan

        How the recommendations were built

        The planner above works from a short, hand-picked list of 38 places. The ranking model behind Treasure Travel was built and tested offline on public Google Maps reviews of Boston from the UCSD Google Local dataset: 2,210 places, 22,287 reviewers and 352,408 reviews.

        1. Split by time

          Keep reviews inside the Boston city boundary. Each reviewer's older reviews train the model; their newest ones are held out for testing.

        2. Baseline signals

          Popularity, a smoothed rating, and TF-IDF similarity between a place's text and the places the reviewer liked before.

        3. Collaborative filtering

          Implicit-feedback ALS factors trained with PySpark on who reviewed what.

        4. Remove popularity

          For each reviewer, regress the ALS scores on log popularity and keep the residual, the taste popularity can't explain.

        5. Choose weights, test once

          Blend weights are chosen on a validation split. Test NDCG@5 is reported once, on held-out reviews.
